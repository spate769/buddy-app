Safety and Guidelines

Buddy is for meeting new people in small groups. Safety comes before everything else, and these are our commitments.

  • Every meetup is at a public place.
  • Everyone has a profile photo, so you know who you're meeting.
  • Groups only, never one-on-one. Women-only groups are available.
  • Report or block anyone in two taps. They're never told who reported them.
  • People see your area and a rounded distance, never your address.

In an Emergency

If you are in danger right now, call 911. Then tell us what happened using the Report button in your group chat.

  • If a meetup feels wrong, leave. You don't need to explain.
  • Move to a busy spot, or ask staff at the venue for help.
  • If you're in crisis or need someone to talk to, call or text 9-8-8 any time (Canada's 24/7 crisis line).

Before You Meet

  • Meet at the public place listed on the event. Don't move the plan to someone's home.
  • Tell a friend where you're going and when you expect to be back.
  • Check your group's photos and social links in the group chat before you go.
  • Get there and home on your own. You don't need to share your address.
  • Leave whenever you want. You don't owe anyone an explanation.

Reporting and Blocking

You can report a person, a group or an event from any group chat or event page. Reports are private: nobody is told who reported them. A person on the Buddy team reviews every report within 24 hours.

Blocking someone removes them from your groups and stops them being placed with you again.

Community Guidelines

  • Be respectful. No harassment, insults or pressure of any kind.
  • Groups, not dates. Buddy is for friendship. Don't use it to pursue someone romantically.
  • Be yourself. Use your real first name and a recent photo of your face.
  • Everyone is welcome. Discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age or background is not allowed.
  • Adults only. You must be 18 or older.
  • Show up, or say so. If you can't make it, tell your group in the chat.
  • Nothing for sale. No selling, recruiting or promoting in groups.

Breaking these guidelines can mean losing access to Buddy.

Privacy

  • We collect only what's needed to match groups: your first name, email, area, age, life stage, interests, and a profile photo. A social link is optional. Gender is optional and only used for women-only groups.
  • Others see your first name, photo, area and interests, plus your social link if you add one. They never see your exact age, email or address.
  • We never sell personal data.
  • You can delete your profile at any time from your profile page.

During the prototype, everything you enter is saved only in your browser on this device.
